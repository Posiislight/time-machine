Time Machine

Rewind an AI agent to the last good moment, without redoing the work.

Every step an agent takes on a Maritime machine is saved as a snapshot. When it goes wrong, pick an earlier moment and continue from a fresh machine. Anything that reached the outside world, such as a payment, an email or a published release, is kept on a list that a rewind never repeats.

This starts a Maritime machine on your account. Machines you start here use your plan's credit.

  1. 1

    Work happens on a machine

    Run commands, or let your agent run them through the API. Each step saves a snapshot.

  2. 2

    Choose a moment

    Click a snapshot. You see what would come back and what would not.

  3. 3

    Rewind

    A new machine starts from that snapshot. Actions already taken stay on record and are never repeated.
